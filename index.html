A data story about Australia · 2022–2025

Live Well, Australia

How lifestyle shapes our health — state by state. Smoking, diet, drinking, activity and weight vary a surprising amount across the country. Do we live longer where we live better?

10.6% of adults smoke daily
23.9% meet the activity guidelines
1 in 4 exceed the alcohol guideline
Geography People Payoff Play
Part 1

The geography of lifestyle

Lifestyle is not spread evenly across the map. The same behaviour can be twice as common in one state as in another. Four different map idioms — a filled choropleth, a proportional-symbol map, a dot-density map and a cartogram — show where Australians are heaviest, where they smoke most, where they drink most, and where diets fall short.

Part 2

Who lives well?

Zooming in on people rather than places. A report-card heatmap compares every state on every factor at once; a dumbbell lays bare the gap between men and women; a socioeconomic gradient shows activity rising with wealth; and the trend line charts smoking's long decline.

Part 3

Lifestyle & wellbeing — the payoff

Do lifestyle choices and health outcomes move together? A scatterplot traces the link between diet and weight across the states, and a radial plot ranks the nation's biggest lifestyle gaps side by side.

Part 4

How Australia plays

Only a quarter of adults meet the full activity-and-strength guideline — yet 86% move in some way. The national AusPlay survey shows what Australians do, how often, and where they are most active.

Notes

  • All health figures are percentages of adults for 2022 unless stated. "Exceeded the alcohol guideline" uses the 2020 Australian Adult Alcohol Guideline.
  • Australia was cropped out of the Natural Earth 1:110m Admin 0 – Countries layer using mapshaper, and the state and territory boundaries were added to build the base map used by every map on this page.
  • The cartogram is schematic: each state keeps its shape but is scaled by its score, so sizes show the measure, not land area. Dot positions within a state are illustrative, not actual addresses.
  • Built with Vega-Lite. Each chart's JSON specification lives in the /js folder of the source repository and is human-readable.
